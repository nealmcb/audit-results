Richmond County sets risk-limiting audit: dice roll Nov. 12, audits start Nov. 13
Nov 6, 2026

Under SB 3EX procedures the county will perform risk-limiting audits; the dice roll to select batches is set for Nov. 12 and the local audit will begin Nov. 13 with observers including the Carter Center.
