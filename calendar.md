# Post-election audit calendar: November 3, 2026 general election

Compiled 2026-11-10 from state and county sources, statutes and news reports. Each row gives its
basis:

- **official**: a 2026 notice, calendar or release from the state or county
- **computed**: a 2026 date worked out from a statute or rule
- **news**: a 2026 news report
- **precedent**: inferred from an earlier election
- **unverified**: not checked against its source text

Treat **computed**, **precedent** and **unverified** dates as provisional until a notice appears.
All times are local to the state unless a zone is given.

## Watch now (Nov 10–24)

These are the events where something can still be captured before it happens: a public draw, a
seed, or evidence published before a seed.

### Nov 6–15

- **WY**: SF 113 hand counts: at its canvass (by Fri Nov 6) each county canvassing board chose tabulators covering about 5% of Election Day ballots; counts due 9 days after the canvass, Sun Nov 15 at the latest. · _computed (W.S. 22-16-103(c)(i); SF 113 § 2(b))_. The act does not require the tabulators to be chosen at random or in public. [Text](https://wyoleg.gov/2026/Enroll/SF0113.pdf)

### Nov 10–12

### Thu Nov 12

- **GA**: SOS RLA kickoff and public dice roll, 10 am ET, South Steps of the State Capitol, Atlanta; open to the public and the media. · _news: [92.1 WLHR, Nov 12](https://921wlhr.com/secretary-of-state-kicks-off-risk-limiting-audit-with-dice-roll-today/); Richmond County elections staff told its board on Nov 6 that "the dice roll to select batches will take place Thursday, Nov. 12 at 10 a.m.", as summarized by [Citizen Portal](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/11902431/georgia/richmond-county/augusta-city/richmond-county-sets-risk-limiting-audit-dice-roll-nov-12-audits-start-nov-1) (an AI summary of the meeting video, at 36:57)_. First statewide RLA under SB 3EX, with 20 contests: governor, lieutenant governor, attorney general, secretary of state, U.S. Senate, all 14 congressional seats, and one more state office chosen at random ("They'll be drawing that out of the hat," Raffensperger told [WSB-TV, Oct 22](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/georgias-secretary-state-touts-vote-audits-ahead-midterms-we-audit-fool-out-it/UCIRZFQHSFGKXBJ3UJS3H5QAHI/)); how and when that draw is made is not announced. @GaSecofState posted SHA-256 hashes of the ballot manifests (d731216443e415c9…) and the machine-counted batch totals (419a77b32fd393e9…) at 16:50:48 and 16:51:24 UTC ([thread](https://x.com/GaSecofState/status/2120922270394982432)), about 1 hour 50 minutes after the roll began; in 2025 and May 2026 they came 8–54 min after it. The files themselves are to be published when the audit completes. The batch retrieval list (601 batches in 159 counties, prepared by VotingWorks; PDF created 12:28 pm ET) shows the office drawn at random was the Commissioner of Agriculture. The seed itself has not been found yet
