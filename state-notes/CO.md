## A model of what citizen verification can achieve

Colorado runs a statewide ballot-level comparison risk-limiting audit, and in a growing number of
counties citizen verifiers go further. With the county's cooperation, they obtain the audit's
evidence before the random seed is drawn (the ballot manifest, a redacted cast vote record and the
list of ballot cards), have it timestamped publicly, and then check the audit boards' work as it
happens. The result is an audit whose findings members of the public can independently reproduce,
not just observe.

See [Citizen Election Verification](https://sites.google.com/view/auditenhancement) for how it
works: the roles of coordinators and verifiers, what counties provide, and how on-premises
verification is done.
