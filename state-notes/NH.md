New Hampshire's audit of the November 3, 2026 general election is done. The Secretary of State's
[audit report](https://www.sos.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt561/files/media/media_document/2026-general-election-audit-report.pdf),
dated November 6, says "all results within expected range". From the report:

- **Selection**: 8 polling places under RSA 660:35, one from each registered-voter quartile of
  the towns and wards using each vendor's devices (4 VotingWorks, 4 Liberty Votes). They were
  chosen on October 16, after the memory cards and election definitions were programmed, by
  entering each quartile's size into "Google's random number generator". The selection is secret
  by law until Election Day, and no seed or other record that would let the public reproduce it is
  given. A ninth device, in Nashua Ward 9, was chosen by the Secretary of State under RSA 660:32.
  Where a polling place had several devices, the one with the most ballots when the notice was
  delivered was audited.
- **Election Day**: Secretary of State staff told each polling place before noon and stayed to
  keep the selected device's ballots separate; the locations were then announced in a press
  release.
- **Method**: on November 4–5, equipment procured by the Secretary of State re-tabulated the
  ballots from each selected device, and the counts were compared. A random sample of 50 ballots
  or 2%, whichever is larger, was examined by hand against the audit equipment's record of each
  ballot.
- **Results**: differences of a vote or two per candidate in several contests (for example,
  Hudson's State Representative race), all called "within expected margins". The report does not
  say what margin is expected.

Pages that are images only make up 46 of the report's 87 pages.
