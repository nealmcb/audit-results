Current & Upcoming Elections

Oregon’s Election Division is your official source for trusted information about elections. We oversee the administration of elections to ensure voting is secure, accessible, and reflects the will of the people. Visit our Election Information page to learn more.

General Election – November 3, 2026

Oregon held a statewide general election on November 3, 2026.

Key Dates

Voter registration deadline: October 13
Counties began mailing ballots to active voters in Oregon: October 14
Last recommended day to return ballots by mail: October 27
Last day to return ballots using a drop box: November 3 at 8 p.m.
Last day for counties to receive valid postmarked ballots by mail: November 10
Last day to resolve ballot challenges: November 24
State election results will be certified: December 10

​​​​​​​ View a printable version of all elections deadlines.

Election Results & Ballot Return Counts

Election results are unofficial until they are certified. Oregon law requires post-election audits in all counties following primary, general, and special elections before the results are final (ORS 254.529). You may view unofficial returns and results as ballot processing continues.

  • Unofficial ballot return data is available now and is updated every business day. The data in this report is fluid and changes as ballots are received and processed.
  • Unofficial results are available at results.oregonvotes.gov and will be updated at regular intervals.
  • Final election results will be certified and made available on December 10.

Contact your county elections office for more information on local post-election processes. Find more information about election security at OregonVotes.gov/integrity.

Results and data from previous elections are available on the Secretary of State’s Historical Election Data webpage.

All active registered voters were mailed a ballot containing the relevant candidates and measures based on the residential address on their voter registration. Statewide races were on every ballot, which included the races for Governor and U.S. Senator this year. There were no statewide ballot measures on the ballot this election. 

Party affiliation does not impact the contents of general election ballots. 

Registered voters may check their voter information and status online using My Vote, or by calling their county elections office.

How Do I Track My ballot?

It takes time for your ballot to travel through the mail to you, and for returned ballots to be processed and signatures verified. 

There are two ways to check the status of your ballot:

  1. You can sign up to receive notification updates about your ballot from outbound to accepted at OregonVotes.gov/track.
  2. OregonVotes.gov/MyVote will have information including:
    • If your ballot has been sent, the page will display a mailed date.
    • If your ballot has been received, the page will display a received date.
    • If you returned your ballot and do not see a date yet, please check back.

Reporting a suspected election law violation

Under 260.345(1), and OAR 165-001-0095, complaints must be from an individual registered to vote in Oregon, and the complaint must be signed. All relevant documentation must be included with the request

Review relevant election laws prior to filing a request. With some exceptions, the Elections Division can only investigate alleged violations of ORS chapters 246 – 260 and Administrative Rule chapter 165. Requests alleging violations outside of the Elections Division's jurisdiction will not be investigated.

If you believe Oregon election law has been violated and are an Oregon registered voter, investigation requests can be filed to the Elections Division using this online form.
