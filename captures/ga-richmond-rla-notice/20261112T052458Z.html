Risk Limiting Audit

Board of Elections Posted on November 05, 2026

The Richmond County Board of Elections will conduct a risk limiting audit of the ballots cast during the November 3, 2026 General and Specula Election beginning at 9:00 AM on Friday, November 13, 2026 at the Board of Elections Warehouse located at 2029 Lumpkin Road, Augusta, GA 30906. Observers are allowed, but access to or the viewing of any ballots cast is not permitted. Please contact the Board of Elections Office at 706-821-2340 for more information
