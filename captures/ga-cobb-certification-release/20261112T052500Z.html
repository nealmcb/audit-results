Cobb County Certifies Results of November 3 General Election/Special Election

The Cobb County Board of Elections and Registration certified the county's results for the November 3, 2026, General Election/Special Election during its meeting on Monday, November 9.

A total of 339,277 ballots were cast in Cobb County, representing a 64.52% voter turnout among the county's 525,871 active registered voters. Cobb County also recorded the highest voter turnout among metro Atlanta's four largest counties in the November 2026 General Election.

The certification follows the completion of required post-election procedures, including reviewing election records, reconciling ballots, and verifying reported results.

Cobb County's certified results will be included in Georgia's statewide certification process. The Georgia Secretary of State is responsible for certifying federal and statewide election results by November 20, 2026.

A total of 26,161 more ballots were cast in 2026, representing an 8.4% increase compared to 2022. Overall voter turnout increased from 61.70% to 64.52%.

Advance Voting turnout increased from 35.74% in 2022 to 43.23% in 2026, with 227,318 Advance Voting ballots cast compared to 181,386 in 2022. This represents a 25.3% increase in Advance Voting ballots cast.

Turnout percentages reflect the share of active registered voters who cast a ballot.

What's Next? Post-Election Audits

Cobb County Elections & Registration will participate in Georgia's post-election audits scheduled for November 13, 16, and 17.

The Risk-Limiting Audit (RLA) involves manually reviewing randomly selected paper ballots to verify the reported outcome of a contest selected by the Secretary of State. Cobb County will also conduct an Enhanced Audit, which uses independent software to review ballot images and compare findings with reported results.

These audits provide an additional layer of verification before statewide certification.
