Cobb County Completes Post-Election Audits of November 3 Election Results

Cobb County Completes Post-Election Audits of November 3 Election Results

Share:

Tuesday, November 17, 2026

Cobb County Elections & Registration completed two post-election audits following the November 3, 2026, General Election/Special Election. Both audits met the state's established statistical standard for a Risk-Limiting Audit, providing additional confirmation of the accuracy of Cobb County's election results.

The Risk-Limiting Audit and Ballot Image Audit took place November 13 and 16–17, following the Cobb County Board of Elections and Registration's certification of results on November 9. Both audits were open to public observation. 

Risk-Limiting Audit

The Risk-Limiting Audit uses a manual count of randomly selected paper ballots and statistical methods to verify reported election outcomes.

For the November 3 General Election, the audit included nine contests. Cobb County was assigned 21 batches of ballots through a statewide random selection process conducted by the Georgia Secretary of State's Office, which included a public dice roll.

Over the course of the audit, 23 two-person teams manually counted 145,244 ballots and compared their findings with the reported totals.

The selected batches included:

  • Advance (Early) Voting: 11 batches
  • Election Day: 9 batches
  • Absentee Voting: 1 batch

The Georgia Secretary of State's Office published the November 2026 General Election Batch Retrieval List, identifying the batches selected for each county.

Ballot Image Audit

The Ballot Image Audit uses scanning technology to read voters' selections on ballot images and compares the findings with reported election results.

A total of 339,277 ballot images were reviewed as part of the audit.

These audits are part of the checks and balances that help ensure accurate election results and provide the public with greater transparency into the election process.

For more information about how election results are reported and certified, visit our Unofficial vs. Official Election Results article.
