# Pennsylvania

[← All states](index.md) · [Calendar](../calendar.md) · [Captures](../captures.md)

Pennsylvania's risk-limiting audit of the November 3, 2026 general election audits the Governor
contest, the only contested statewide office, using Arlo. The Department of State rolled the dice
for the seed on November 13 in a livestream with Secretary of the Commonwealth Al Schmidt, as
Department staff took turns rolling 10-sided dice.

The seed, as shown on screen in the Department's
[recording](https://www.facebook.com/PADepartmentofState/videos/2172973120761958):
`48828428706800623218`. The first digit was entered 4:50 into the recording and the last at 7:47.
The recording's timestamp is 16:58:02 UTC; if that marks the start of the stream, the digits were
entered from about 17:02:52 to 17:05:49 UTC (12:02–12:05 pm ET). Frames showing the seed are in
`evidence/PA/`.

We found nothing published before the roll that would let the public check the sample: no ballot
manifests, cast vote records or batch tallies, and no hashes of them. Publishing those, or at least
their hashes, before the dice are rolled would show that they could not have been adjusted once the
sample was known.

Wanted: the seed as posted on the audit page, to confirm it; Arlo's list of counties and batches;
and the manifests and results when published.
