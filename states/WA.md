# Washington

[← All states](index.md) · [Calendar](../calendar.md) · [Captures](../captures.md)

Washington runs ballot-comparison risk-limiting audits in Arlo, at a 5% risk limit, on November 19:
Supreme Court Justice Position 7 statewide in all 39 counties; Congressional District 1 in King and
Snohomish; and one contest each in Kitsap (Prosecuting Attorney), Spokane (Legislative District 6
State Representative Position 2), Thurston (Sheriff) and Whatcom (City of Bellingham Proposition
2026-06).

The Elections Division rolled the seed in public on Friday, November 6, and streamed it on YouTube
([recording](https://youtube.com/live/Jp2coFs32m8), unlisted). The captions, kept in
`evidence/WA/`, show the 20 dice called one at a time from about 23:02:50 to 23:04:20 UTC (YouTube
gives the stream's start as 23:02:09 UTC), and the read-back of the seed `04912937865602606747`,
which matches the one posted on the Secretary of State's site.

The contests were then drawn by lot, after the seed was known: the statewide contest from eight
slips (three initiatives and five Supreme Court positions), the congressional district from all
districts except the 5th, which was left out as too close to audit, and one contest for each of the
four counties from lists of their contests. A draw by lot from slips can't be checked afterwards the
way a seed can, and drawing after the seed means the contests could in principle be chosen knowing
which ballots each would sample.

Nothing was said about the ballot manifests or cast vote records, and none were published, nor
hashes of them, before the roll. Reproducing the sample needs those files; we have asked the
Elections Division for them and for the times they were uploaded to Arlo.

Wanted: the manifests and CVRs for the six audits, their upload times, and Arlo's sample lists.
