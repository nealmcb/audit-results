# Wyoming

[← All states](index.md) · [Calendar](../calendar.md) · [Captures](../captures.md)

Wyoming runs two kinds of post-election audit in 2026:

- **The standing audits** of W.S. 22-6-130 and 22-11-109, which continue.
- **A one-time hand count** under SF 113 (2026 Budget Session, Enrolled Act No. 61, Section 2),
  for the 2026 primary and general elections only. Section 2 is not codified, so it does not
  appear in Title 22. Text: https://wyoleg.gov/2026/Enroll/SF0113.pdf

The SF 113 hand count:

- **Ballots**: Election Day ballots from the tabulators the county canvassing board chooses,
  totaling as close to 5% of the county's Election Day ballots as possible. The act does not
  say the tabulators must be chosen at random, or in public.
- **Races**: one federal and one statewide race picked by the Secretary of State, who must tell
  each county clerk by close of business on the Wednesday after the election (Nov 4). The races
  may differ by county. Every legislative race on the ballot is also counted, and the canvassing
  board may add races.
- **Who**: audit boards of at least three electors, no more than two from the same party,
  appointed by the county clerk. They count each mark, undervote and overvote as the tabulator
  would under the ballot's instructions.
- **When**: within nine days after the county canvass. The canvass must meet by the first Friday
  after the election (W.S. 22-16-103(c)(i)), Nov 6 this year, so the counts are due by Nov 15 at
  the latest.
- **Reporting**: inconsistencies or errors go to the Secretary of State. Section 2(d) says "not
  less than nine (9) days after" the canvass, apparently meaning "not later than". The Secretary
  of State reports on the hand counts to the joint corporations, elections and political
  subdivisions interim committee by May 1, 2027.

Wanted: the races the Secretary of State picked, each county's tabulator selection and how it
was made, and the hand-count results.
