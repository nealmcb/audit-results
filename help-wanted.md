# Help wanted

These need a person: a phone call, a browser, a records request, or someone at the audit. If you
can take one, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/nealmcb/audit-results/issues) saying which,
and add what you find there. Please include the source: who said it, when, and a link
or document.

## Capture pages before draws

These pages refuse automated fetches. A saved copy (File > Save Page As) with the time you saved
it, attached to a GitHub issue, is the most useful thing you can do before each
state's draw. Browser-driving AI agents such as Claude Cowork or Claude in Chrome can do the same
job. For posts on X, Grok can search and quote them directly, and Gemini can find and timestamp
moments in YouTube recordings, such as when the dice are rolled. Please say which tool you used.

- Georgia: https://sos.ga.gov/page/elections-audit-information and https://sos.ga.gov/news
- Georgia, Clayton County's audit notice: https://www.claytoncountyga.gov/news/risk-limiting-audit-november-3-2026-general-election/
- Rhode Island: https://elections.ri.gov/elections/risk-limiting-audit-center
- Montana: https://sosmt.gov/elections/
- Wisconsin: https://elections.wi.gov/
- Arizona: https://azsos.gov/node/876
- Texas: https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/

## Observe an audit

Many audits are open to the public. Observer notes (who was there, what was counted, any
discrepancies, how ballots were stored and retrieved) are valuable evidence. See the
[calendar](calendar.md) for dates; the nearest are Georgia's county hand counts (Nov 13–17),
Pennsylvania's dice roll (Nov 13), Washington's county RLAs (Nov 19) and DC's manual audit
(Nov 21).
